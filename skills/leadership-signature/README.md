# Leadership Signature Skill

This skill helps convert personal leadership reflection into practical leadership behavior.

## Purpose

Identify how a person naturally leads, where that leadership style helps or hurts, and how to develop a stronger future leadership self.

## Use Cases

- Founder self-awareness
- Manager development
- Delegation improvement
- Team meeting design
- Family business role clarity
- Conflict response
- Leadership story writing

## Core Outputs

- Leadership signature statement
- Leadership story
- Immunity-to-change map
- Development experiments
- Future-self milestones
