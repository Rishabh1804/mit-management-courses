# Skill: Leadership Signature

## Capability

Use structured reflection to identify a learner's leadership signature and convert it into practical leadership behavior.

## Inputs

- Current role
- Business context
- Leadership challenges
- Past formative experiences
- Feedback from others
- Desired future role

## Workflow

1. Identify current leadership patterns.
2. Extract strengths and blind spots.
3. Map formative experiences.
4. Diagnose resistance to change.
5. Design provisional leadership experiments.
6. Define future-self milestones.
7. Produce a leadership signature and development plan.

## Output Format

- Leadership signature statement
- Current pattern summary
- Development edge
- Immunity map
- Experiments
- 30-60-90 day leadership plan
