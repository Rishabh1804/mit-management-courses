# Leadership Signature Examples

## Example 1: Delegation

Situation: The leader wants to delegate but keeps pulling tasks back.

Useful frameworks:

- Immunity to Change
- Provisional Self

Possible development experiment:

Delegate one outcome with clear success criteria, but do not prescribe the method. Review after completion.

## Example 2: Family Business Role Clarity

Situation: Family expectations and business authority are overlapping.

Useful frameworks:

- Family Systems Model
- Leadership Signature

Possible development experiment:

Separate family discussion from business decision meeting. Clarify role, decision rights, and next action.

## Example 3: Meeting Leadership

Situation: Meetings run long and end without decisions.

Useful frameworks:

- 4-CAP+
- Future Self

Possible development experiment:

Open each meeting with purpose, decision needed, owner, and expected output.
