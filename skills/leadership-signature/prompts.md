# Leadership Signature Prompts

## Discovery Prompt

Help me identify my leadership signature using my current role, business context, leadership challenges, and formative experiences.

## Reflection Prompt

Ask me structured questions to uncover the leadership patterns I learned from family, work, authority, conflict, and responsibility.

## Immunity Prompt

Help me build an Immunity to Change map for a leadership goal I keep failing to act on consistently.

## Future Self Prompt

Help me define the leader I want to become in one year and convert that into 30, 60, and 90 day milestones.

## Business Application Prompt

Apply my leadership signature to this business situation and tell me what behavior, communication style, and decision rhythm I should use.
