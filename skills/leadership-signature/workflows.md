# Leadership Signature Workflows

## Workflow 1: Build a Leadership Signature

1. Collect role, context, and current challenges.
2. Identify three moments of effective leadership.
3. Identify three moments of leadership friction.
4. Extract repeated strengths and risks.
5. Draft a leadership signature statement.
6. Test the statement against current business situations.

## Workflow 2: Diagnose a Leadership Growth Block

1. Define the improvement goal.
2. List behaviors that block the goal.
3. Identify hidden commitments.
4. Name the assumptions behind those commitments.
5. Design a small safe experiment.
6. Review what the experiment reveals.

## Workflow 3: Apply Signature to a Business Situation

1. Define the situation.
2. Identify stakeholders.
3. Identify what the situation needs: visioning, relating, inventing, or sensemaking.
4. Choose leadership behavior aligned with the user's signature.
5. Define the communication, decision, and follow-up rhythm.
