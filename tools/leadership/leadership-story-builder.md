# Leadership Story Builder

## Purpose

Use this tool to turn reflection into a coherent leadership story.

## Structure

### 1. Origin

What early experiences shaped how I understand authority, responsibility, trust, and conflict?


### 2. Crucible

What difficult experience changed how I lead?


### 3. Current Signature

How do I lead today?


### 4. Development Edge

What pattern am I now trying to improve?


### 5. Future Self

Who am I becoming as a leader?


### 6. Commitments

What will I practice consistently?
