# Feedback Request Template

## Purpose

Use this template to gather feedback on leadership patterns from trusted colleagues, family members, managers, or team members.

## Message Template

I am working on understanding and improving my leadership style.

Could you answer these three questions honestly?

1. What is one strength you consistently see in the way I lead?
2. What is one pattern that may limit my leadership effectiveness?
3. What is one behavior I should start, stop, or strengthen?

A short reply is enough. Specific examples would be especially useful.

## Reflection After Receiving Feedback

What repeated themes appeared?


What surprised me?


What will I test in my behavior?
