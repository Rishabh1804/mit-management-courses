# Authenticity Paradox

## Purpose

The Authenticity Paradox describes the tension between staying true to the current self and growing into a new leadership role.

## Core Idea

Leadership growth often feels uncomfortable because new behaviors may initially feel unnatural. The risk is that a person protects a familiar identity instead of practicing the behaviors required by a larger role.

## Reflection Questions

- What leadership behaviors feel authentic to me now?
- What new behaviors feel artificial but may be necessary?
- Where am I using authenticity as a reason not to grow?
- What version of myself am I trying to protect?
- What new behavior can I practice without betraying my values?

## Business Application

Use this framework when moving from operator to manager, from manager to leader, or from family member to formal business leader.
