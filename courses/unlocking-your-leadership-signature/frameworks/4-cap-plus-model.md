# 4-CAP+ Model

## Purpose

The 4-CAP+ model provides a way to examine leadership capacity across core activities.

## Capabilities

### Visioning

Creating direction, aspiration, and a picture of a better future.

### Relating

Building trust, connection, credibility, and shared commitment.

### Inventing

Designing new ways of working, solving problems, and creating useful change.

### Sensemaking

Interpreting ambiguous situations, identifying patterns, and helping others understand what is happening.

## Plus Element

The plus element is the personal grounding that allows the leader to use these capabilities authentically and adaptively.

## Diagnostic Questions

- Where am I strongest today?
- Which capability do others rely on me for?
- Which capability is underdeveloped?
- Which capability does my current business context require most?

## Business Application

Use 4-CAP+ to diagnose whether a leadership situation needs more direction, more trust-building, more innovation, or better interpretation of complexity.
