# Provisional Self Framework

## Purpose

The Provisional Self Framework helps a leader experiment with new leadership behaviors before those behaviors feel fully natural.

## Core Idea

Leadership identity develops through practice. A leader can try provisional versions of the future self, observe what works, adapt, and integrate the useful parts.

## Practice Loop

1. Identify a future leadership behavior.
2. Choose a low-risk context to test it.
3. Practice the behavior deliberately.
4. Observe the effect on self and others.
5. Keep, adapt, or discard the behavior.
6. Repeat until the behavior becomes integrated.

## Business Application

Use this for delegation, assertive communication, meeting leadership, conflict handling, and strategic thinking routines.
