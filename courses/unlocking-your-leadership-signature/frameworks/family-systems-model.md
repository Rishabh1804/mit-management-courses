# Family Systems Model

## Purpose

The family systems lens helps explain how early relational patterns can shape current leadership behavior.

## What to Examine

- How authority was expressed in the family system
- How disagreement was handled
- How responsibility was distributed
- How recognition and criticism were given
- How conflict, loyalty, control, and autonomy showed up

## Reflection Questions

1. What leadership behaviors did I learn early?
2. Which of those behaviors still help me?
3. Which behaviors now limit me?
4. What situations trigger old patterns?
5. What new leadership response do I want to practice?

## Business Application

This is especially useful in family business settings where personal history, authority, ownership, and operating responsibility often overlap.
