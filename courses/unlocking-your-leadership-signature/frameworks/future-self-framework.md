# Future Self Framework

## Purpose

The Future Self Framework helps translate leadership aspiration into a concrete image, milestones, and behavior plan.

## Core Questions

- Who do I want to be as a leader one year from now?
- What do people trust me for in that future state?
- What difficult situations can I handle better?
- What capabilities must I build?
- What habits must I stop, start, or strengthen?

## Output Template

> My future leadership self is known for ___. I create value by ___. I handle pressure by ___. I build trust through ___. My next milestones are ___.

## Business Application

Use this framework to connect personal leadership development to company-building, succession, delegation, team culture, and strategic execution.
