# Immunity to Change

## Purpose

Immunity to Change is a diagnostic framework for understanding why sincere improvement goals often fail.

## Core Structure

1. Improvement goal
2. Behaviors working against the goal
3. Hidden competing commitments
4. Big assumptions behind those commitments
5. Small safe experiments to test the assumptions

## Template

| Column | Prompt |
|---|---|
| Improvement goal | What do I genuinely want to improve? |
| Blocking behaviors | What do I do or avoid doing that prevents progress? |
| Hidden commitments | What am I also committed to protecting? |
| Big assumptions | What do I assume will happen if I change? |
| Experiments | What small test can challenge the assumption safely? |

## Business Application

Use this when a leader repeatedly says they want change but keeps recreating the same pattern: over-control, delayed delegation, conflict avoidance, unclear communication, or inconsistent follow-through.
