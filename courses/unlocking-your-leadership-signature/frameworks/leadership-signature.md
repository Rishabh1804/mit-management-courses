# Leadership Signature

## Definition

A leadership signature is the recognizable pattern by which a person leads. It combines experience, personality, strengths, values, learned behaviors, and aspirations.

## Core Questions

- What do people experience when I lead?
- What strengths do I naturally rely on?
- What leadership behaviors create trust?
- What leadership behaviors create friction?
- What kind of leader am I trying to become?

## Practical Tool

Use this framework to produce a concise leadership signature statement:

> I lead best when I ___, by using ___, in service of ___. I must watch for ___, and I am developing ___ so that ___.

## Business Application

Use the signature statement to guide meeting behavior, delegation style, feedback conversations, hiring expectations, and founder or manager operating rhythm.
