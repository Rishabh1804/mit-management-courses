# Syllabus

## Course Structure

The course is organized into six weekly modules.

## Module 1: Identifying Your Leadership Signature

Purpose: Begin the leadership story by exploring personal leadership patterns and the leadership signatures of others.

Repo output:

- Initial leadership signature inventory
- Leadership role model notes
- First leadership signature statement

## Module 2: Analyzing the Past to Become a Better Leader in the Future

Purpose: Use the family systems model to understand how past experiences shape present leadership behavior.

Repo output:

- Past leadership stories
- Family system influence map
- Feedback request template

## Module 3: Analyzing Your Developed Self

Purpose: Explore crucibles, developmental stages, and the 4-CAP+ model.

Repo output:

- Crucible analysis
- 4-CAP+ assessment
- Leadership story draft

## Module 4: Diagnosing Immunity to Change

Purpose: Apply the Immunity to Change framework to a specific leadership goal.

Repo output:

- Improvement goal
- Competing commitments
- Big assumptions
- Small experiments

## Module 5: First Steps to Developing as a Leader

Purpose: Study the Authenticity Paradox and use the Provisional Self Framework to experiment with new leadership behavior.

Repo output:

- Authenticity paradox reflection
- Provisional leadership experiments
- Behavior practice plan

## Module 6: Being the Leader of Tomorrow: Your Future Self

Purpose: Define the future leadership self, identify milestones, and polish the final leadership story.

Repo output:

- Future-self statement
- Milestone map
- Final leadership story
- Leadership development plan
