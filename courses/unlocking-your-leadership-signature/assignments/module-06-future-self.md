# Module 06 Assignment: Future Self

## Objective

Define the leader you are becoming and convert that image into milestones.

## Future Self Questions

1. Who do I want to be as a leader one year from now?
2. What should my team trust me for?
3. What decisions should I handle better?
4. What behavior must I stop?
5. What behavior must I start?
6. What behavior must I strengthen?
