# Module 01 Assignment: Leadership Signature Inventory

## Objective

Identify the first version of your leadership signature.

## Prompts

1. List three leaders you respect. What is recognizable about the way each one leads?
2. List three moments where people trusted your leadership. What did you do?
3. List three moments where your leadership created friction. What pattern do you see?
4. What strengths do you naturally use when leading?
5. What do you want people to experience when you lead?

## Output

Draft this statement:

> I lead best when I ___, by using ___, in service of ___. I must watch for ___, and I am developing ___.

## Business Application

Choose one active business situation and describe how this leadership signature should change your next action.
