# Module 05 Assignment: Provisional Self

## Objective

Use leadership experiments to practice a future version of yourself before it feels fully natural.

## Authenticity Paradox Reflection

Answer:

1. What leadership behavior feels natural but may be limiting you?
2. What new behavior feels unnatural but may be required by your next role?
3. Where are you using authenticity to stay comfortable?
4. What values must remain intact while you experiment?

## Provisional Self Experiment

Choose one behavior to test for one week.

| Experiment | Context | Intended Effect | Risk | Debrief |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | |

## Examples

- Ask more questions before giving instructions.
- Delegate the outcome, not the method.
- Open a meeting with context and decision criteria.
- Address conflict directly but calmly.
- Let a team member present instead of speaking for them.

## Output

A one-week leadership behavior experiment and reflection.
