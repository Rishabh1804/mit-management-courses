# Module 04 Assignment: Immunity to Change

## Objective

Diagnose why a leadership improvement goal is not yet translating into consistent behavior.

## Step 1: Improvement Goal

Write one leadership goal that matters now.

Example:

> I want to delegate more clearly and stop pulling work back to myself.

## Step 2: Blocking Behaviors

List what you do or avoid doing that blocks the goal.

## Step 3: Hidden Commitments

Identify what you may be protecting.

Examples:

- Avoiding loss of control
- Avoiding conflict
- Avoiding appearing inexperienced
- Avoiding dependency on others

## Step 4: Big Assumptions

Write the assumptions beneath the commitments.

Example:

> If I delegate fully, quality will drop and I will be blamed.

## Step 5: Experiments

Design one small safe experiment to test the assumption.

## Output

Complete an immunity map and one leadership experiment.
