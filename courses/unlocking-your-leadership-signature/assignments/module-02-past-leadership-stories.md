# Module 02 Assignment: Past Leadership Stories

## Objective

Understand how past family, work, and authority experiences shape current leadership behavior.

## Prompts

1. What did your family system teach you about authority?
2. How was conflict handled around you when you were younger?
3. What responsibilities did you take on early?
4. Which old patterns still help your leadership?
5. Which old patterns now limit your leadership?

## Story Exercise

Write two short stories:

- A past experience that strengthened your leadership
- A past experience that created a leadership blind spot

## Feedback Request

Ask one trusted person:

> What leadership pattern do you see in me that I may not fully notice?

## Output

A one-page past-to-present leadership map.
