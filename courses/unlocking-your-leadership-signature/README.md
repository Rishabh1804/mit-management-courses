# Unlocking Your Leadership Signature

This course folder converts the MIT Sloan Executive Education program into reusable leadership tools and applied business workflows.

## Course Focus

The course helps a learner identify, articulate, and develop their leadership signature: the practical expression of their experience, personality, strengths, stories, and future leadership goals.

## Primary Outputs

- Leadership signature statement
- Leadership story draft and final version
- Personal leadership development plan
- Immunity-to-change map
- Future-self milestones
- Reusable Leadership Signature skill card

## Module Sequence

1. Identifying Your Leadership Signature
2. Analyzing the Past to Become a Better Leader in the Future
3. Analyzing Your Developed Self
4. Diagnosing Immunity to Change
5. First Steps to Developing as a Leader
6. Being the Leader of Tomorrow: Your Future Self

## Repo Use

Use this folder to convert the course into a working leadership development system for real business contexts: family business, delegation, team meetings, conflict resolution, and founder operating style.
