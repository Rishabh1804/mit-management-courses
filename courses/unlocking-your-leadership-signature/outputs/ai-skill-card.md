# Skill Card: Leadership Signature

## Skill Name

Leadership Signature

## Source Course

Unlocking Your Leadership Signature

## Purpose

Help a learner identify, articulate, develop, and apply their leadership signature in real business contexts.

## When To Use

Use this skill for leadership development, founder operating style, delegation, meeting leadership, family business roles, conflict resolution, team culture, and management behavior change.

## Inputs

- Current role and responsibilities
- Business context
- Leadership challenges
- Past stories and formative experiences
- Feedback from others
- Development goals

## Process

1. Identify current leadership patterns.
2. Map formative experiences.
3. Analyze strengths and blind spots.
4. Diagnose immunity to change.
5. Design provisional leadership experiments.
6. Define future leadership self.
7. Produce a leadership story and development plan.

## Outputs

- Leadership signature statement
- Leadership story
- Immunity map
- Development plan
- Business application notes

## Example Prompt

Help me identify my leadership signature using my current business situation, my management challenges, and my past leadership experiences.
