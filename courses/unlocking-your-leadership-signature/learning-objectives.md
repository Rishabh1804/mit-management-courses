# Learning Objectives

By the end of this course conversion, the learner should be able to:

1. Communicate a clear understanding of their leadership signature.
2. Explain how their leadership signature can make them a more inspiring business leader.
3. Choose one core leadership development approach to improve their signature.
4. Define future leadership goals and concrete next steps.
5. Reflect on the type of team leader they want to become.
6. Apply leadership-signature thinking to real business situations.

## Applied Business Objectives

In this repo, those course objectives are translated into practical business capabilities:

- Lead meetings with clearer intent.
- Delegate without ambiguity.
- Build trust with family and non-family team members.
- Diagnose recurring leadership friction.
- Create a personal management operating style.
- Turn reflection into measurable behavior change.
