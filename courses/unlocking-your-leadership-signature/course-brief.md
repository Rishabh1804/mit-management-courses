# Course Brief

## Course

Unlocking Your Leadership Signature

## Provider

MIT Sloan Executive Education, delivered with Emeritus.

## Faculty Anchor

Deborah Ancona, MIT Sloan School of Management.

## Duration

6 weeks, with an estimated 4 to 6 hours per week.

## Core Promise

The course develops an introspective and practical approach to leadership. The learner identifies their leadership signature, studies how their past shapes current leadership behavior, diagnoses barriers to change, and creates a future-facing leadership story.

## Practical Interpretation

For this repo, the course becomes a leadership operating system. It should help the user:

- Understand their default leadership style
- Improve communication and delegation
- Design better meetings and operating rhythms
- Handle family-business complexity
- Develop managers and teams
- Build a clear leadership story for future growth

## Final Course Output

A polished leadership story supported by a development plan and practical leadership tools.
