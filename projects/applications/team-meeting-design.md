# Team Meeting Design

## Purpose

Use leadership signature thinking to design meetings that match the leader's strengths while improving clarity, trust, and follow-through.

## Meeting Leadership Questions

1. What is the purpose of this meeting?
2. Is this a visioning, relating, inventing, or sensemaking meeting?
3. What decision or output must exist by the end?
4. Who owns the next action?
5. What leadership behavior should I practice in this meeting?

## Leadership Experiment

Choose one behavior to practice during the meeting:

- Ask before telling.
- State the decision rule.
- Clarify ownership.
- Summarize disagreement neutrally.
- Close with next actions.
