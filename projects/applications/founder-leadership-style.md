# Founder Leadership Style

## Purpose

Use the Leadership Signature course to define a founder or senior operator's personal management style.

## Founder Pattern Questions

1. What do people come to me for by default?
2. Where do I create clarity?
3. Where do I create dependency?
4. Where do I over-control or under-specify?
5. What leadership behavior must change as the business scales?

## Operating Style Statement

I create value as a founder by ___. My strongest leadership mode is ___. I create risk when I ___. My next-stage leadership behavior is ___.

## Practical Applications

- Decision rights
- Delegation design
- Weekly meeting rhythm
- Escalation rules
- Manager development
- Strategic communication

## 30-Day Founder Experiment

Choose one behavior to test:

- Stop solving first; ask for the team's diagnosis.
- Define the decision owner before discussion.
- Delegate the outcome and constraints, not the entire method.
- Review progress through metrics and exceptions instead of constant checking.
- Make one recurring meeting decision-oriented rather than update-oriented.

## Review

At the end of 30 days, answer:

1. What changed in team behavior?
2. What did I find uncomfortable?
3. What improved in clarity, speed, or ownership?
4. What should become a permanent leadership habit?
