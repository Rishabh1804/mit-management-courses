# Delegation System

## Purpose

Apply leadership signature and Immunity to Change concepts to improve delegation quality.

## Delegation Diagnosis

Common delegation failure modes:

- Outcome is unclear.
- Authority is unclear.
- The leader delegates the task but not the decision right.
- The leader prescribes the method too tightly.
- Follow-up rhythm is missing.
- Quality expectations are implicit.

## Leadership Reflection

1. What am I afraid will happen if I delegate fully?
2. What hidden commitment does that fear reveal?
3. What small experiment can test whether that fear is accurate?
4. What support does the other person need to succeed?

## Practice Rule

Delegate outcomes, constraints, and check-points. Do not delegate vague expectations.
