# Conflict Resolution

## Purpose

Use leadership signature concepts to handle conflict with more clarity, composure, and accountability.

## Conflict Diagnosis

Before responding, clarify:

1. What is the actual business issue?
2. What is the emotional issue?
3. What is the role confusion?
4. What decision or agreement is needed?
5. What pattern am I repeating?

## Useful Frameworks

- Family Systems Model
- Authenticity Paradox
- Provisional Self Framework
- 4-CAP+

## Conversation Structure

1. Name the issue neutrally.
2. Separate facts from interpretations.
3. State the effect on work or trust.
4. Ask for the other person's view.
5. Define the decision or behavior needed.
6. Agree on the next action.

## Practice Script

I want to discuss this clearly without making it personal. The issue I am seeing is ___. The effect is ___. I may be missing something, so I want to understand your view. What I think we need to decide is ___.

## Reflection

After the conversation, note:

- What did I avoid?
- What did I handle better than usual?
- What did the other person need from my leadership?
- What will I do differently next time?
