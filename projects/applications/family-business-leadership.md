# Family Business Leadership

## Purpose

Apply leadership signature concepts to family business situations where family roles, ownership roles, and operating roles overlap.

## Common Patterns

- Authority is implicit instead of explicit.
- Conflict is avoided to preserve family harmony.
- Decision rights are unclear.
- Responsibility is inherited rather than designed.
- Feedback becomes personal instead of operational.

## Useful Frameworks

- Family Systems Model
- Leadership Signature
- Immunity to Change
- Future Self Framework

## Application Questions

1. Which family pattern is shaping the business situation?
2. What role am I playing: family member, owner, manager, or operator?
3. What decision rights need to be clarified?
4. What leadership behavior would create trust without avoiding accountability?
5. What conversation needs to be separated from family emotion and treated as a business issue?

## Practical Action

Create a role clarity note before any difficult family-business conversation:

- Situation
- People involved
- Business issue
- Family sensitivity
- Decision owner
- Desired outcome
- Next step
